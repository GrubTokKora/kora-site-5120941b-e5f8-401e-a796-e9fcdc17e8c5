The Skin Journey

A plan first. Hands-on work when the plan calls for it.

  1. 01

    VIRTUAL SKIN JOURNEY CONSULTATION

    $165/60 min

    You send clear photos and a short intake before we meet. Then a full hour on video: what you use, how you use it, what your skin does week to week. Afterwards you get a written, step-by-step routine. Products are bought separately, and that’s intentional.

  2. 02

    Virtual Skin Journey Follow-up

    $95/30 min

    We look at how your skin has responded, keep what’s working and adjust one change at a time, so you always know what caused what.

  3. 03

    In-studio treatment

    Priced per treatment

    When the plan needs hands-on work, such as extractions or a peel, you come to the studio in downtown Hartford.

Questions

Before your first visit

How should I prepare for my appointment?

Arrive with a clean face, and tell me about any skin concerns before we start. Tell me what you use at home too, even if it’s “just a cleanser”, so I can plan the treatment around your skin.

What aftercare do you recommend after a facial?

Cleanse gently and moisturize, skip heavy makeup, and keep out of the sun while your skin settles.

Do you accept digital payments?

Yes. I accept card payments and digital wallets.

Is the studio wheelchair accessible?

Yes. The studio at 200 Columbus Blvd, 22nd Floor, Hartford, CT 06103 is wheelchair accessible.

Is there parking at the studio?

Yes. Valet parking is available when you arrive.

The studio is at 200 Columbus Blvd, 22nd Floor, Hartford, CT 06103, in downtown Hartford. It is open seven days: Monday to Friday 3:00 PM - 7:00 PM, Saturday 11:00 AM - 5:00 PM and Sunday 3:00 PM - 6:00 PM.

How long have you been doing this?

I’ve worked in Hartford for nine years, on my own, and I work with a lot of melanin-rich skin. The person who reads your intake is the person who treats your skin.

What happens during a facial?

Every facial starts with a conversation about what your skin is doing and what you use at home. Then comes the treatment itself, and I explain each step before I do it, so nothing comes as a surprise.
