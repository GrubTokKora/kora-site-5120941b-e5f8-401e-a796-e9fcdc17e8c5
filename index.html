FAQ

Good to know

Which treatment should I book first?

Use “What would you like to change?” above to match your main concern to a treatment. Every service begins with a personal skin assessment, and if you'd like guidance before booking, start with a virtual consultation.

What should I expect during a facial?

Expect a personalized consultation followed by a pampering treatment that leaves your skin refreshed and rejuvenated.

How should I prepare for my appointment?

Arrive with a clean face, and share any skin concerns so your treatment can be fully customized.

What aftercare do you recommend?

Gentle cleansing and moisturizing, plus avoiding heavy makeup and sun exposure right after your treatment.

Do you treat darker skin tones?

Yes. Vanessa works with every skin type and tone, and many clients choose her for her experience with melanin-rich skin, hyperpigmentation and acne.

Is there parking, and is the studio accessible?

Valet parking is available, and the studio is wheelchair accessible.

How do payments and deposits work?

In-studio treatments are secured with a $30 deposit, and virtual consultations are paid at booking. Cash, cards and digital wallets are accepted, and you can pay over time with Cherry.
