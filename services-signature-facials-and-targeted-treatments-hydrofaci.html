What it does

Three things your skin responds to fast

This is a non-invasive facial built on exfoliation, gentle extraction and serum infusion.

  1. 01

    Exfoliation

  2. 02

  3. 03

    Serum infusion

The serum

Chosen for your skin that day

New England weather swings show up on skin. Winter indoor heat can leave you tight and flaky; summer sun can push dehydration and uneven tone. So the serum changes with you.

  • Hydrating

    For when indoor heat has your skin feeling tight.

  • Antioxidant

    For when you’ve been in the sun more than you meant to.

  • Brightening

    For when you’re noticing dullness and uneven tone.

I work with all skin types, and I’m especially careful with melanin-rich skin, where unnecessary irritation can leave marks behind.

Questions

Results, downtime and event timing

Can I get a HYDROFACIAL with serum the week of my wedding or event?

Yes, and it’s one of my go-to recommendations for that timeline because it’s gentle and gives an immediate glow. If it’s your first time, booking 7 to 10 days before your event gives you a cushion, just in case your skin decides to be extra sensitive that week.

How long do results from a HYDROFACIAL with serum last?

Most people notice the glow and hydration right away, and it often looks best for about a week. How long it holds depends on your skin and your routine. If you want longer-lasting changes in texture and congestion, I usually recommend staying consistent with a short series and then monthly maintenance.

Is there downtime after a HYDROFACIAL with serum and when can I wear makeup?

Downtime is minimal for most people, which is why it’s popular before events. If you can, give your skin the rest of the day makeup-free so the serum can sit and do its job. If you need makeup for work or an event, you can usually wear it later the same day.

Can a HYDROFACIAL with serum remove blackheads and unclog pores?

It can help a lot. The exfoliation plus gentle extraction is designed to lift debris and clear congestion, including blackheads. If your pores are very stubborn, we’ll talk about a realistic plan and the right spacing between visits so you keep improving instead of starting over each time.

Is a HYDROFACIAL with serum worth the price compared with other facials?

If you want a visible glow with minimal downtime, it tends to feel worth it, because it combines exfoliation, extraction and targeted serum infusion in one 65-minute visit. It’s also a strong option when you don’t want to gamble on redness right before an event. And I’ll be honest: you’re paying for results and a plan that makes sense for your skin, not a sales pitch.
