What the follow-up covers

How your skin is responding, and what to do next

Your first follow-up lets us evaluate how your skin is responding and determine whether your routine needs to be adjusted. It is a concise, evidence-based update, not a full treatment or an in-person exam.

What happens in the session

We look at how your skin has responded since your last plan. I review your current routine, note how each product has gone down, and pick out simple adjustments.

Recommendations are practical and prioritized: small changes first, and larger steps only if they’re needed. That keeps things manageable around New England’s seasons and a working week.

How I assess progress

I compare what you’ve observed, any photos you send, and how you’ve actually used your products since the last plan. I focus on the signals you notice: tolerance, irritation, whether improvement is consistent, and whether the routine fits your life. Cold winters and humid summers come into it too, because they change how skin behaves.

I also weigh the trade-offs: layering more products versus simplifying, and whether a product’s benefit justifies its cost or its complexity. If you commute through Union Station, spend time outdoors or prefer an evening routine, that shapes which changes are realistic enough to keep up.

Adjusting your home routine

I adjust the routine when the follow-up shows a clear need, or when a product isn’t tolerated the way we expected. Typical changes are a different product order, swapping one formulation for another, or cutting a step that’s doing the same job as another.

Each change comes with a reason, how long it should take to see an effect, and what to watch for, so you can decide with clarity. Then we settle what comes next: carry on with the revised routine, book another follow-up, or consider an in-studio service.

What to bring

Your current product list, notes on what you’ve noticed, and recent photos of your skin if you have them. With those in hand, the 30 minutes go on decisions rather than catching up.

Questions

About the follow-up

What should I prepare for the follow-up session?

Bring a list of every product you’re using, notes on how your skin responded, and recent photos if you have them. These let us evaluate tolerance and progress efficiently during the 30-minute virtual call.

How do you measure whether my routine is working?

We compare what you’ve observed, any photos you provide, and your product use since the last plan. I focus on the signals you notice: tolerance, irritation, consistency of improvement, and fit with your lifestyle. New England’s cold winters and humid summers come into it, because they commonly change how skin behaves. I also weigh trade-offs such as layering products versus simplifying, and whether a product’s benefit justifies its cost or complexity. If you commute through Union Station, spend time outdoors or prefer evening routines, those practical constraints shape which adjustments are realistic and sustainable.

Can you change products during the virtual follow-up?

Yes. I can recommend swaps, order changes or simplifications based on how your skin has responded. Recommendations are prioritized so you make one or two changes at a time rather than overhauling your routine.

Is a virtual follow-up as useful as an in-person visit?

A virtual session is highly useful for reviewing how well your routine is working and adjusting products based on your real-world results. It isn’t a substitute for hands-on treatment, but it’s an efficient way to monitor progress and plan next steps.

How soon should I schedule another follow-up?

It depends on the adjustments we made and how quickly your skin, or the products, show change. Typically I recommend monitoring for a few weeks, then booking another check if results are unclear or further changes are needed.

What is the cost and session length?

The Virtual Skin Journey Follow-up is 30 minutes and $95. It’s a fixed-length virtual appointment focused on evaluating progress and updating your routine with clear next steps.

Are follow-ups available after my virtual consultation?

Yes. Follow-up check-ins are there if you want support as you introduce products and track progress, and I’ll guide you on the best next step for your skin. Skincare products are purchased separately and aren’t included in the fee. The VIRTUAL SKIN JOURNEY CONSULTATION is $165 for 60 minutes.
