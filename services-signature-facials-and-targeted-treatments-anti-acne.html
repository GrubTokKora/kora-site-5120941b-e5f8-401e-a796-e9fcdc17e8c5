Before you come

How to prepare

The goal is simple: come in with skin that’s calm enough for me to work effectively.

  • In the 3 to 7 days before, I generally recommend pausing strong exfoliants and retinoids, so your barrier isn’t already feeling raw.
  • Arrive makeup-free if you can. It saves time and lets me see what’s really happening in your pores, texture and inflammation.
  • Tell me what you’re using at home, even if it’s “just a cleanser”, plus any sensitivities or recent changes to your routine.
  • Tell me about any prescription products, especially prescription retinoids, and absolutely let me know if you’re on Accutane or have been recently. That changes what we can safely do.
  • If you’re pregnant, tell me that too.

I’m honest about what your skin needs, and I’ll explain everything so you feel comfortable and confident before we begin.

During the appointment

What happens in the 60 minutes

Each step depends on what your skin is doing that day. This is the order I work in.

  1. 01

    A real conversation

    I ask what you’re seeing, what you’ve tried, and what your skin tends to do in different seasons. Hartford humidity can push oil production up fast, so we talk about lightweight hydration and sunscreen habits that won’t feel heavy or greasy.

  2. 02

    Cleanse and assess

    I cleanse thoroughly, then look closely at congestion, inflamed breakouts and areas that are quietly clogging under the surface. If you have deeper bumps, I’ll tell you what a facial can do and what takes consistency over time. I work with a lot of melanin-rich skin, and I’m mindful about not triggering unnecessary irritation that can lead to discoloration.

  3. 03

    Exfoliation

    This may include a chemical exfoliant that helps loosen dead skin and congestion, so extractions are cleaner and less traumatic. You might feel tingling or warmth. That’s common. I keep checking in so you’re not silently white-knuckling through it.

  4. 04

    Extractions

    The part most first-timers worry about. I take my time, I focus on what’s ready, and I don’t force areas that will just swell and get angry.

  5. 05

    Calming, acne-focused care

    I apply calming and acne-focused products to reduce inflammation and support recovery. Depending on what your skin needs, we may use targeted masks or other treatment steps that help with active breakouts and overall texture.

  6. 06

    Hydration and SPF

    We finish with hydration and SPF, because unprotected healing skin can stain, especially in brighter months. You leave with simple aftercare.

Will it hurt?

Afterwards

“They can be uncomfortable, but they shouldn’t feel brutal.”

Expect some redness

Most people feel some tenderness and see mild redness after extractions, usually for a few hours. Some people stay a little pink into the next day. Sometimes congestion that was already developing comes to the surface. That’s purging, and it’s different from a true reaction.

Protect the results

Gentle care for 48 to 72 hours, no picking, and SPF. Consistent follow-up visits matter too, so we’re not starting over each time. If breakouts keep returning between visits, a VIRTUAL SKIN JOURNEY CONSULTATION builds the plan you follow at home.

Questions

About the anti-acne facial

Does an ANTI-ACNE FACIAL TREATMENT hurt?

It can be uncomfortable in moments, especially during extractions, but it shouldn’t feel aggressive. I explain what I’m doing as we go, I check in often, and I focus on what’s ready instead of forcing stubborn areas. Most people feel some tenderness and see mild redness afterward, usually for a few hours and sometimes into the next day.

Will my skin get worse after an ANTI-ACNE FACIAL TREATMENT?

Sometimes you’ll see short-term purging, which is when congestion that was already developing comes to the surface. That’s different from a true reaction. Mild redness is also normal right after. The best way to protect results is gentle care for 48 to 72 hours, no picking, and consistent follow-up visits so we’re not starting over each time.

How do I prepare for my first ANTI-ACNE FACIAL TREATMENT?

I generally recommend pausing strong exfoliants and retinoids for 3 to 7 days beforehand so your skin barrier stays calm. If you can, arrive makeup-free. And please tell me what you’re using at home, plus any prescriptions, sensitivities, or recent changes in your routine, so I can choose the safest, most effective approach for your skin that day.

How long is an ANTI-ACNE FACIAL TREATMENT and what happens during it?

It’s 60 minutes. We start with a consultation, then I cleanse and assess your skin. Next I exfoliate and soften congestion, perform extractions as appropriate, apply calming and acne-focused treatment steps, and finish with hydration and SPF. You’ll leave with simple aftercare so your skin stays protected as it recovers.
