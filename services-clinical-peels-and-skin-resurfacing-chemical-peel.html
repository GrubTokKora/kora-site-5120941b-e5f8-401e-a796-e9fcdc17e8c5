The treatment

What a peel does, and what it asks of you.

$230 / 60 min Clinical Peels & Skin Resurfacing

A chemical peel speeds up skin cell turnover so your complexion looks clearer, smoother and more even. I use advanced formulations designed to gently exfoliate, reveal brightness and refine texture, without taking you out of your routine when we choose the right strength.

My Chemical Peel service is 60 minutes and $230.

What it can help with

The big benefits are brighter skin, smoother texture and a more even tone. A peel helps lift dull surface buildup and speeds up cell turnover, which is why it can improve the look of these over time:

  • Hyperpigmentation
  • Sunspots
  • Acne marks
  • Fine lines

It often helps most with discoloration left behind by breakouts and sun exposure. Peels work by encouraging the skin to shed damaged layers, so the surface looks clearer and more even as fresh skin comes through.

Is it safe for my skin?

A peel can be safe for darker skin tones when the peel type and strength are chosen carefully and aftercare is followed closely. I work with a lot of melanin-rich skin, so I’m very mindful here: smart peel selection, then simple, protective post-peel steps, so you get results without unnecessary irritation.

If you’re a little nervous before a peel, that’s normal. I explain the why behind each step, answer your questions, and keep you grounded in what’s normal and what’s not.

What recovery looks like

Recovery depends on the strength of the peel.

  1. Light peel

    Often little to no visible peeling.

  2. Medium peel

    Flaking commonly shows around days 3–7.

  3. The weeks after

    Your tone and texture can keep improving for several weeks as your skin finishes its turnover.

One peel, or a series?

Many people notice improvement after one peel, especially in brightness and texture. For stubborn hyperpigmentation, acne marks or ongoing congestion, a series usually gives the best momentum, since results build with consistent treatments and proper aftercare.

If you’re deciding between treatments: MICRODERMABRASION suits discoloration that is more superficial. When pigment is deeper or stubborn, I’ll talk you through options like a chemical peel, so you’re not spinning your wheels.
