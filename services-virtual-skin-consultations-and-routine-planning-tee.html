Teen Virtual Consultation

Yes, I can help your teen’s acne without a visit: in a 30-minute video call I sort out what’s actually going on with the breakouts, set up a routine that’s safe for teenage skin, and map a realistic follow-up timeline, so nobody is guessing for months. You join from home; I’m in Hartford.

Who it’s for

Teens aged 14 to 18 dealing with acne, dryness and uneven texture. It’s a good fit if your teen has tried “everything” and still feels stuck, or has bounced between too many products and routines that felt harsh and didn’t match their skin.

It’s also a strong choice for melanin-rich skin, where advice needs to respect how dark skin can react to irritation. If your teen has eczema as well as acne, we can build a routine that supports the skin barrier instead of fighting it.

It’s discreet, calm, and focused on real progress, not pressure.

Parents, or a private session

Parents are welcome to join. Some families sit in for the first few minutes to cover goals, allergies, budget and household routines. Some teens open up more when it’s just us. I’m comfortable either way, and we’ll set it up in whatever way feels respectful and age-appropriate.

How it works

  1. Photos before the call

    Once you’re booked, I’ll tell you exactly which photos to send: clear and makeup-free, in natural light, straight-on, left side, right side, and a close-up of the area that bothers you most. A clean, well-lit photo saves us time and helps me give better guidance.

  2. The video call

    We talk through what your teen sees in the mirror and what they’ve already tried. I tighten up the basics — cleanser, moisturizer, sunscreen — then add acne steps that suit their skin type and comfort level. This isn’t a random product list and a “good luck”.

  3. A routine and a timeline

    You leave with a routine your teen can actually keep on busy school mornings, an explanation of what each step is for, and a realistic follow-up timeline so you know what should change first.

What changes, and when

Acne usually improves by doing fewer things, more consistently. So I build plans that feel doable for a teen, not a twelve-step routine that collapses after three days. Texture and oiliness tend to shift first, then active breakouts, then stubborn marks.

  • 2–4 weeks

    Early wins many teens notice: less redness, fewer new breakouts, skin that feels calmer.

  • 8–12 weeks

    Deeper improvement, especially where there are dark marks or long-term congestion.

Straight answers on products and medicine

  • The fee is for my time and the plan. Skincare products are purchased separately, and I don’t push them.
  • I don’t prescribe medication. If I hear something that sounds beyond what non-prescription skincare should handle, I’ll say so plainly and recommend a dermatologist evaluation.
  • Hormonal shifts can still cause flares, so I focus on a steady plan your teen can keep up with, with clear check-in points.

When hands-on care makes sense, I’ll tell you: a TEEN FACIAL or extractions in the studio, or looping in dermatology. Many teens do well starting virtually, then coming in when their skin is ready.

Questions

What parents and teens ask

Can a virtual consultation help with hormonal acne in teens?

Yes, it often helps, because we can calm the routine down, reduce irritation and track flare patterns over time. Hormonal shifts can still cause flares, so I focus on a steady plan with clear check-in points. If what I see suggests medical support is needed, I’ll recommend a dermatologist evaluation.

Do parents need to be present?

No. Parents can join at the start if you want help covering goals and household routines, or your teen can meet with me privately. We’ll set it up in a way that feels comfortable and age-appropriate.

What photos should we send before the consultation?

Clear, makeup-free photos in good natural light: straight-on, left side, right side, plus a close-up of the main concern area, whether that’s forehead, cheeks, chin or jawline. For body acne, include that area too. Skip filters so I can see texture and inflammation accurately.

How soon will my teen see improvement?

Many teens notice early changes in 2–4 weeks, like less redness and fewer new breakouts. Deeper improvement often takes 8–12 weeks, especially with dark marks or long-term congestion. I’ll give you a timeline based on what your teen is using and what their skin is doing.

Is a virtual consultation as effective as an in-person facial for teen acne?

They do different jobs. A virtual consultation is best for building the daily routine and correcting the habits that drive acne. An in-person facial helps when we need hands-on care like extractions or professional treatments. Many teens start virtually, then come in when their skin is ready for that next step.
