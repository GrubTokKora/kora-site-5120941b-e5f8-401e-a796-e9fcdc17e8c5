What I remove, and why your makeup notices

Peach fuzz, the fine vellus hair on your face, is completely normal. The trouble is what it does to makeup. It catches product, especially thicker foundation, tinted moisturiser and setting powder, and the dead skin sitting around it gives makeup dry patches to cling to. That’s when your base separates, looks textured in photos, or starts streaking by lunchtime.

I use a specialised surgical scalpel to carefully exfoliate the surface, removing dead skin buildup and fine vellus hair together. That combination is the reason makeup tends to lay more evenly right away. It doesn’t rely on chemical exfoliants: clearing the surface physically lets light reflect better, so skin looks brighter, and it lets your serums and moisturisers absorb more effectively afterwards.

  • Immediate smoothing

    Removes dead skin and fine hair for an instantly smoother, brighter complexion without long recovery.

  • Better product absorption

    A clearer skin surface lets serums and moisturisers penetrate more effectively after treatment.

  • Improved texture appearance

    Can help reduce the look of uneven texture and surface scarring when it’s part of a care plan.

  • Minimal downtime

    Results are visible right away, and most clients go back to their day as usual.

What happens in the 60 minutes

We start with a conversation. Bring a list of the skincare you’re using now and anything that’s been bothering you lately, so I can check the treatment fits your goals and your skin that day.

The treatment itself is steady and methodical: precise, superficial exfoliation across the face with a controlled hand technique, so we smooth texture without irritating your skin. You’ll see the result as soon as I’m finished. I close with products suited to freshly exfoliated skin and clear aftercare guidance, so you leave knowing exactly what to do next.

Who it suits, and when I’ll suggest something else

Dermaplaning is suitable for most skin types. It’s at its best on dry, dull or textured skin, and when your main goals are smoother texture, brighter-looking skin and makeup that stops catching. It can also improve the look of shallow acne scarring and uneven texture, though how much depends on the depth of the scarring and on your skin, so I assess first and tell you honestly what to expect.

I’ll also be straight with you when it isn’t the right move. Dermaplaning usually isn’t a good choice if you have:

  • active inflammatory or cystic acne, or irritated breakouts
  • open lesions
  • recent use of isotretinoin

In those cases a CHEMICAL PEEL or another resurfacing option such as MICRODERMABRASION may make more sense, or I may suggest you see a dermatologist for medical acne care first.

Aftercare that keeps the result

Downtime is minimal, but aftercare matters. I’ve just cleared away the outer layer, so your skin is fresh and more receptive. That’s a good thing, as long as you keep it calm.

  1. First 24–48 hours

    • Skip intense heat and heavy sweating.
    • Keep products simple: gentle cleanser, moisturiser and SPF.
    • If you’re planning makeup, keep it light and let your skin settle first.

  2. Next 5–7 days

    • Hold off on strong exfoliants and aggressive actives.
    • Be gentle with scrubs, brushes and at-home devices.

  3. 1–2 weeks

    • Be strict with SPF. Hartford sun and the reflection off the riverfront can sneak up on you, even when it doesn’t feel hot.

How long it lasts, and how often to come back

Results commonly last about 3 to 6 weeks, and a lot of my clients book every 4 to 6 weeks to keep that smooth texture and easier makeup application. The right rhythm for you depends on your skin, your goals and the season, since dry winters and humid summers both change how skin behaves, so we set it together.

I’m open seven days with weekday appointments until 7:00 PM, so maintenance can fit around work along Columbus Boulevard or a weekend in town.

Questions

About dermaplaning

What exactly does dermaplaning remove from the skin, and why?

Dermaplaning removes the superficial layer of dead skin cells and fine vellus hair using a surgical scalpel. That clears surface texture, increases smoothness and improves how your skincare is absorbed, without relying on chemical exfoliants.

Does dermaplaning remove peach fuzz?

Yes. Dermaplaning removes vellus hair (peach fuzz) and the outer layer of dead skin, which is why skin tends to feel immediately smoother and makeup lays more evenly.

How long is a dermaplaning appointment, and what does it cost?

A dermaplaning appointment is 60 minutes and a single session is $210. That time covers a consultation, the treatment itself, and brief aftercare instructions so you leave knowing what to do next.

Will dermaplaning help acne scars and uneven skin texture?

It can improve the appearance of surface irregularities and some shallow scarring by smoothing the skin’s top layer. How much it helps depends on scar depth and your individual skin, so I assess first and set realistic expectations.

Is dermaplaning safe if I have acne?

It depends on the type of acne. If you have active inflammatory or cystic acne, open lesions, or irritated breakouts, dermaplaning usually isn’t the best choice because it can aggravate the area. If your acne is calm and you’re dealing more with leftover texture, we can talk through options and choose the safest plan.

Is there downtime after dermaplaning?

Downtime is minimal for most clients, and many return to daily activities the same day. Because the outer layer of skin is freshly exposed, brief aftercare and sun protection are recommended to support healing and comfort.

How long after dermaplaning can I wear makeup?

Many people prefer to wait until the next day so the skin can settle, especially if you’re prone to sensitivity. If you do wear makeup later the same day, keep it light and non-irritating, use clean brushes or sponges, focus on hydration, and follow the aftercare guidance I give you after your session.

How often should I get dermaplaning to keep skin smooth?

Results commonly last about 3 to 6 weeks, and a lot of my clients schedule every 4 to 6 weeks to keep that smooth texture and easier makeup application. The right rhythm depends on your skin, your goals and the season, since Hartford’s dry winters and humid summers change how skin behaves, so we set your schedule together at your consultation.

What should I avoid after dermaplaning?

Plan on strict SPF for at least 1 to 2 weeks, and keep skincare gentle. Avoid strong exfoliants for 5 to 7 days, and skip intense heat or heavy sweating for 24 to 48 hours to reduce the chance of irritation.
