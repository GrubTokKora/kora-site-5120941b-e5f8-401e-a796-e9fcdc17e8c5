The teen approach

How the Teen Facial works

Teenage skin has its own concerns, from oiliness and sensitivity to early breakouts. This facial is built for them, and for teaching good habits early.

Every service and price
  • TEEN FACIAL 45 min $140
01

Skin analysis and a real conversation

Every Teen Facial starts with a short intake and a close look at the skin, so I know its type and what’s bothering your teen: oiliness, sensitivity, early breakouts. We talk about lifestyle and the products already in the bathroom, so the treatment fits their skin instead of following a one-size-fits-all routine.

It’s also where I set realistic expectations, and where parents or guardians can ask their questions if they’re with us.

02

Gentle cleansing, exfoliation and hydration

I cleanse, exfoliate carefully and hydrate, using products chosen for young skin. The aim is to lift surface impurities and support the skin’s natural moisture without stripping it. I watch how the skin responds throughout and keep it gentle.

How strong the exfoliation is, and how often, is a judgement I make against sensitivity and skin type. Young skin can behave differently from season to season, and New England’s cold winters and humid summers change oil, barrier and aftercare, so I adjust for that too.

03

A home routine they’ll keep

The teaching is built into the session. I show the simple daily steps and explain why each product is there, so your teen understands their own skin. The advice stays short and practical, and I shape it around school days and weekends.

At the end of the 45 minutes I outline a simple follow-up plan and answer any last questions.

Plainly, what it is and isn’t

This is facial care, not medicine. For mild breakouts I adjust the cleansing and exfoliation. If acne is severe or cystic, I’ll tell you so and recommend seeing a dermatologist alongside the facials. I don’t diagnose or prescribe.

Before you come

Bring a list or photos of the products your teen already uses, and any skin concerns you want me to look at. Arriving with a clean face helps.

What your teen leaves with

  • Age-appropriate care

    Gentle methods and products suited to adolescent skin, avoiding overly aggressive treatments.

  • Personal guidance

    A plan built on a focused skin analysis, with practical at-home recommendations.

  • Habits that last

    Simple, sustainable steps that support healthy skin without complicating their routine.

  • Times that fit

    Evening and weekend appointments for commuting families and students in downtown Hartford.

Questions parents ask

About the Teen Facial

Is the Teen Facial safe for acne-prone teenage skin and breakouts?

The treatment is guided by the assessment, with gentle products and a measured approach to avoid irritation. For mild breakouts I adjust the cleansing and exfoliation. If acne is severe or cystic, I recommend seeing a dermatologist alongside facial care. I don’t provide medical diagnoses or prescriptions.

Will a parent need to come to the appointment?

It depends on age and comfort. Parents are welcome to join the consultation if that helps, and I include parents or guardians in the aftercare conversation. Ask about specific age guidelines when you book so you can plan.

How long is a Teen Facial and what does it cost?

45 minutes, $140. That covers the skin analysis, the treatment itself and a short review of at-home care.

Can we book after work, after school or at the weekend?

Yes. Weekdays run 3:00 PM - 7:00 PM, Saturdays 11:00 AM - 5:00 PM and Sundays 3:00 PM - 6:00 PM. The studio is in downtown Hartford near Union Station and the I-91 exits, valet parking is available, and it’s wheelchair accessible.

Will you recommend gentle products and a home routine?

Yes. Every session includes simple, age-appropriate recommendations for your teen’s skin type and lifestyle. I keep the routine short and manageable, without complicated steps or too many products.

Is it relaxing as well as educational?

It’s meant to be both. Teens can relax during the treatment while learning practical skincare steps they can keep doing at home.
