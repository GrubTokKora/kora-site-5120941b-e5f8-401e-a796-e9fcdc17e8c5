Services

Every treatment, what I do in it and what it costs. Each one has its own page with the detail and the questions people ask me about it.

Book a time

Signature Facials & Targeted Treatments

Deep-cleansing facials and targeted treatments for acne, hydration and glow.

  • HYDROFACIAL with serum

    Length: 65 min Price: $260

    Exfoliation, gentle extraction and a serum I choose for what your skin is doing right now: hydrating, antioxidant or brightening. Visible results without a long recovery window, which is why people book it before an event.

    About the HYDROFACIAL with serum

  • ANTI-ACNE FACIAL TREATMENT

    Length: 60 min

    We start with a real conversation. Then I cleanse, exfoliate, take my time over extractions without forcing anything, and finish with calming acne-focused care, hydration and SPF. I work with a lot of melanin-rich skin and I’m careful not to trigger irritation that leads to discoloration. Pause strong exfoliants and retinoids for 3 to 7 days beforehand.

    About the ANTI-ACNE FACIAL TREATMENT

  • TEEN FACIAL

    Length: 45 min Price: $140

    Cleansing, exfoliating and hydrating for adolescent skin, with a skin analysis first and products gentle enough for young skin. Teens leave with a simple routine they can actually keep at home.

    About the TEEN FACIAL

Clinical Peels & Skin Resurfacing

Professional peels and resurfacing to refine texture and brighten skin.

  • DERMAPLANING

    Length: 60 min Price: $210

    I exfoliate the surface with a specialized surgical scalpel, removing dead skin and fine vellus hair. Skin feels smoother straight away, products absorb better and makeup lays more evenly, with minimal downtime.

    About DERMAPLANING

  • MICRODERMABRASION

    Price: $230

    Fine crystals remove dead surface cells to smooth texture, brighten dullness and soften the look of fine lines and sun damage. I work carefully, especially on melanated skin: the goal is glow without unnecessary irritation.

    About MICRODERMABRASION

  • CHEMICAL PEEL

    Length: 60 min Price: $230

    A peel speeds up cell turnover so your complexion looks clearer, smoother and more even. When I choose the right strength for your skin, it brightens and refines texture without taking you out of your routine.

    About the CHEMICAL PEEL

Virtual Skin Consultations & Routine Planning

Video consultations for a personal routine, product guidance and teen care.

  • VIRTUAL SKIN JOURNEY CONSULTATION

    Length: 60 min Price: $165

    For when you’ve tried so many products and it still isn’t clicking. You send clear photos and a quick intake, we spend the full hour on video, and afterwards you receive a written, step-by-step routine with guidance on how and when to introduce each product. Products are purchased separately, and that’s intentional.

    About the consultation

  • Virtual Skin Journey Follow-up

    Length: 30 min Price: $95

    We review how your skin is responding, go through your current routine and how you’ve reacted to each product, adjust where it’s appropriate, and set your next steps.

    About the follow-up

  • Teen Virtual Consultation

    Length: 30 min Price: $100

    For teens aged 14 to 18 dealing with acne, dryness or uneven texture. I help sort out what’s going on with breakouts, set up a routine that’s safe for teenage skin and map a realistic follow-up timeline. Parents are welcome to join, or teens can meet with me privately.

    About the Teen Virtual Consultation
